Synchronous communication (OpenFeign)

Sequence diagram of the “apply for a job” scenario: Lina (candidate #4) applies to job #1 from the mobile app. The request is followed from the gateway to the database, through Eureka and the two OpenFeign calls.

The steps

Synchronous path (1 → 11)

  • 1–2 the gateway checks the JWT and picks the route /api/applications/** → lb://CANDIDATURE.
  • 3–4 it finds the instances in Eureka and forwards the request to one of them.
  • 5–7 candidature resolves CANDIDAT and JOB, then reads the candidate and the job (OpenFeign).
  • 8–9 business rules, then INSERT into candidature_db.
  • 10 publishes ApplicationSubmitted, without waiting.
  • 11 answers 201 Created with Location: /api/applications/6.

Error cases

  • 401 / 403 at the gateway: missing token or insufficient role.
  • 404 candidate or job not found (FeignException.NotFound).
  • 409 job closed or application already exists.
  • 400 application date in the future.
  • 503 CANDIDAT or JOB unavailable (service stopped, not in Eureka).

For the implementation details, see Workshop 3 (OpenFeign) and the README of the candidature microservice.
