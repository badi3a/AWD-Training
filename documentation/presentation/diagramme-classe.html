Reading the diagram

Associations

  • A candidate has at most one address (0..1 — 0..1).
  • A candidate submits 0..* applications; an application concerns exactly one job.
  • A job belongs to one category; a category groups 0..* jobs.
  • An application can lead to one interview (0..1).
  • Applications and interviews trigger notifications.

Business rules

  • A candidate applies only once to the same job → 409.
  • Applications are accepted only for a job with available = true → otherwise 409.
  • applicationDate is optional (today by default) and never in the future.
  • Candidate e-mail and category name are unique.

Enumerations

  • ApplicationStatus: SUBMITTED, WITHDRAWN.
  • MeetingStatus: TO_SCHEDULE, SCHEDULED, DONE, CANCELLED.
